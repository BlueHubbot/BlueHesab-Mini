## Question
Table 10-2-ت gives W-09 charts 2–4 and W-12 only in words, so the fixture cannot be built without inventing numbers (AG-05, AG-08). What exact data should they be?
## Context (links only; no pasted spec)
`docs/design/ui-spec/10-style-guide-and-fixtures.md` table 10-2-ت rows W-09, W-12; DS-04, DS-06, DS-07; table 10-3-الف rows `ResponsiveList` (W-12, PF-02); `lib/fixtures/src/worst_set.dart`; T-022 non-goals; ADVISOR_REPLY-006 Action 3; T-025 (this PR).
Missing: chart 2 ("one series that crosses zero from W-02 to W-01"): number of points and the values between; chart 3 ("all values zero"): how many series and months; chart 4 ("one point"): its value; W-12 ("200 rows, W-05 and W-01 alternating, descending dates"): is each row W-05 title + W-01 amount, or do rows alternate a W-05 row with a W-01 row (and then what is the other field of each), the first date, the step, kind/category/account.
## Options
A) Erratum (Product/Contracts) with exact data, proposed: chart 2 = one series of 12 months, value(m) = W-02 + (m − 1) × 2 × |W-01| ÷ 11 rounded toward zero, with month 12 set to W-01 exactly (passes zero between m = 6 and 7); chart 3 = five series × 12 months, all 0; chart 4 = one series, one month (m = 1), value W-01; W-12 = row i (0…199): even rows have title W-05 and the `normal` amount 320 000 toman, odd rows have the `normal` title of table 10-2-ب row 1 and amount W-01 (so long titles and long amounts alternate), date = 2 Aban 1405 − i days, expense, category `food`, account `cash`. Cost: one small spec PR; risk low.
B) Simpler erratum: chart 2 = two points [W-02, W-01]; chart 3 = one series × 12 zeros; chart 4 = [W-01]; W-12 = every row title W-05 and amount W-01, date 2 Aban − i days. Cost: same; risk: W-12 then has no "alternation", which the spec text names.
C) Leave them out of `app_fixtures` until the chart and list tasks (M2) and let those tasks write their own data in the test (DS-02 allows a test to bring its own data over `normal`). Cost: none now; risk: two agents may build different "worst" data.
## Recommendation
A, decided by Product/Contracts with the Advisor's choice of exact numbers, because DS-02 wants one shared set and the M2 chart/list goldens need it; any exact choice is better than none.
## What happens if no answer
AG-16 default: W-09 charts 2–4 and W-12 stay out of `lib/fixtures/`; no golden or test uses them. Safe until the first `ResponsiveList` or chart task (M2).
