No packet on `main` is free: T-010 and T-013 depend on T-012 (PR #21, owner, ESC-002); T-017 (PR #27) and T-019 (PR #30) are in open PRs. Builder-Platform changes only the on-device step of `spike-share.yml` (and, if needed, `android_device_ready.sh`): a hang = the attempt hit the per-attempt time limit (exit 124 or 137) after `Installing build/app/outputs/flutter-apk/app-debug.apk` appeared and no test result line was printed. On every hang, attempt 1 and 2, the step prints `adb devices -l`, the last 300 lines of `adb logcat -d` and the last 200 lines of the emulator log. A hang on attempt 1 is retried once with `flutter test -v` and a `::warning` naming "hang after install"; a hang on attempt 2 fails with `::error`. Still at most 2 attempts in total; the DDS and "No supported devices found" retries stay as they are; any other failure stops at once. Interpretations (D0): (1) "no test result line" = the log has no `All tests passed!`, `Some tests failed` or ` +N:` progress line; `No tests ran` is accepted as a positive marker but not required, because a SIGKILL (exit 137) prints nothing; (2) the step limit goes from 20 to 30 minutes so two full attempts plus two device waits fit (job limit 60 unchanged); (3) "3 consecutive green runs" = three `workflow_dispatch` runs of `spike-share.yml` on the branch head, all with the `android` job green, counted after the PR's own run; a run that needed the hang retry is green but is counted towards the ADVISOR_REPLY-005 "2 of 10" follow-up.
